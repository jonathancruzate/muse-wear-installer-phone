# v1.0.1 — rename: label drops the "Phone" suffix

**Built 2026-11-11.** Jonathan's direction (2026-11-11 01:56 EST):
"Just called it Muse Wear Installer" — the phone app's
user-facing name drops the "Phone" suffix and is now exactly
**Muse Wear Installer**, the same product name as the watch app.
The pair shares one name by his direction; they live on different
devices and keep distinct packages.

## What changed (and only this)

- `app/res/values/strings.xml`: `app_name` —
  "Muse Wear Installer Phone" → **"Muse Wear Installer"** (this
  is the `android:label` source; verified by aapt2 badging below).
- `app/build.sh`: `VERSION_CODE` 1 → **2**, `VERSION_NAME`
  1.0.0 → **1.0.1**.

## What deliberately did NOT change

- **Package:** `com.jonathancruzate.metamusewearinstallerphone`
  (identity/upgrades intact; v1.0.1 installs in place over v1.0.0).
- **Project directory** `~/workspace/muse-wear-installer-phone/`
  and **keystore** (same cert, SHA-256
  `af8372b47a49e30043c90db3066556f5fc062e06559cf4539c8701968fdd1fce`).
- **Device identity** `wear-installer` / wearbridge lane.
- **APK filename:** `MuseWearInstallerPhone-v1.0.1.apk` — the
  build script derives it as `MuseWearInstallerPhone-v<VERSION_NAME>.apk`;
  the stem is a filename, not the label, so it stays.
- **Code strings other than the label**, per the rename's scope —
  including the User-Agent strings, which are not user-facing:
  `MuseWearInstallerPhone/1.0` (WorkerClient) and
  `MuseWearInstallerPhone/wear-install` (WearInstallRouter
  download UA). The in-app screen title and the foreground-service
  notification title literals likewise keep their v1.0.0 form in
  this build; only the launcher label changed.
